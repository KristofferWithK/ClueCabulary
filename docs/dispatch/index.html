21 Aug · 22:05

H3 was two summary lines, not a card — now expanded. The “chess bot” idea is real and its shape is a chess engine's: you cannot precompute clues per board (about 1037 possible 18-card boards from 900 words), so you precompute an evaluation — 225 KB of word vectors — and search the clue candidates at runtime.

Two findings came with it. The objective function already exists in prose: prompts.ts tells the model to score every non-target against its candidate clue and reject the clue if any of them fits better — H3 is that paragraph implemented. And it shares its wordlist and its licence question with L1, so the dictionary's source should not be chosen without the clue engine's needs on the table.

Worth knowing what it replaces: today's offline companion clues mok1, mok2, mok3 until one passes the legality check. Not bad clues — not clues.

21 Aug · 21:20

The grammar replaces the story, and the nine chapters are written. docs/grammar-da.md — en/et and the glued definite · plurals · present tense and V2 · questions and ikke · the past · adjective agreement · pronouns and the sin/hans trap · subordinate clauses · modals and register. The eight unwritten rides are cancelled; Sønderborg's survives as an option beside chapter 1.

It is machine-checked. scripts/validate-grammar.mjs verifies every article claim against words.da.json and gates each chapter to the vocabulary the player actually has — both mutation-tested, both wired into npm run verify. What it cannot check is naturalness and the irregular edges, so a Danish speaker still has to read it.

English is never spoken — on the ride or in the review. That drops half of S2's bake and ~13 MB. And T3 opened: each city's example sentences drill the rule taught on the ride in, which means S2 now waits behind T3 or the bake is paid for twice.
