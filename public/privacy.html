900words privacy policy

Last updated 2026-08-20

900words is a Danish vocabulary game. There is no account, no sign-up and no way for the app to know who you are. This page says exactly what leaves your device and why, and it is short because very little does.

Your progress stays on your device

Every word you learn, every round you play, and every setting lives in the app's local storage on your phone. It is never uploaded anywhere. The one way it leaves the device is the backup button in Settings, which writes a file wherever you choose; the app never sees it again unless you restore it. Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data) deletes everything, because everything is here.

What is sent when Casey plays

Casey, the AI companion, is played by a language model. To produce a clue, a guess, a translation or the post-round story, the app sends the words on the current board, the clues and guesses exchanged this round, and whatever you type into the clue and look-up fields to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which forwards it to an AI model provider and returns the reply. These requests carry no name, no account and no contact details — there are none to carry. We do not store the content of requests; the model provider processes them to produce the answer under its own terms. Casey is never shown your secret key, so what you send is what both players can already see, plus what you typed.

One random number, for rate limiting

To keep the free service affordable, the app generates a random installation ID on your device and sends it with each AI request. Our server counts requests per ID per day and refuses further ones past a daily cap. The ID is random, is generated and stored only on your device, means nothing anywhere else, and identifies an installation — not a person. The daily counts expire on their own.

What is not involved at all

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, no cookies beyond the local storage described above. Word audio is ordinary static files the app downloads and caches; the app has no microphone access and records nothing.

Changes and contact

The app is developed in the open; this policy's history is the file's history in the repository. Questions and concerns: open an issue on GitHub.
